# Evidence DOX

## Purpose

Own durable artifacts that justify gate decisions.

## Ownership

Accepted contact sheets, metric reports, gate checklists, exception records, and comparison summaries.

## Local Contracts

- Evidence must identify phase/gate and date/checkpoint.
- Preserve accepted evidence; superseded evidence may be removed when a newer artifact fully replaces it and history remains in Git.
- Do not store third-party reference media here unless redistribution is permitted.

## Work Guidance

Use subfolders by gate when evidence volume grows, e.g. `gate-01/`, `gate-02/`.

## Verification

A gate cannot be marked complete in `docs/STATE.md` unless its required evidence exists here or is referenced by an exact repository path.

## Child DOX Index

None.

TOPOLOGY_AUDIT.json is the reproducible evidence-link/precondition audit for
listed current-build connections. It supplements manual direction/landing review
in native registers. A topology audit PASS is separate from overall Gate1 PASS.
